## Window

- **A new layout**, after VS Code and JetBrains: a title bar across the top, and panels under it. The chat sits in the darkest one, where the work is; the left panel and the right pane are a step lighter.
- **The activity bar:** Threads down the left edge, with Search, Source control, Automations and Plugins as views still to come, and Settings at the bottom. Clicking an item opens its view beside the icons; clicking it again (or ⌘B) narrows the panel back to the icons.
- **New thread** is the **+** in the Threads header.
- **The title bar** is shorter and holds back and forward, the thread's title and its tools. Open in is one button split in two.
- **Panels move faster:** the sidebar and the right pane slide about 40% quicker.

## Composer

- **It stays at the bottom**, from a new thread on; "What should we work on?" sits in the room above it.
- **Plainer:** one border, a brighter fill, less rounded, shorter, and bare icons for send, stop and +.
- **Resume a session** is under **+**, next to Attach files.

## Right pane

- The new tab's search field is as tall as the rows under it and darker, so it stands out.
