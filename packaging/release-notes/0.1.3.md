## Sidebar

- **Pin threads.** Right-click a thread and choose **Pin Thread**: it stays at the top of the sidebar, and keeps its pin when you close and resume it.
- **Sections.** Threads are grouped under **Pinned**, **Today**, **Yesterday**, then one header per earlier day ("3 Nov"). Writing in an older thread doesn't move it. ⌘-numbers and ⌘⌥↑/↓ follow the order on screen.
- **A better right-click menu**: compact rows with each item's shortcut on the right, and a clear highlight under the pointer. Hovering it no longer highlights the threads behind it.

## Terminal

- The terminal pane **slides in and out** (⌘J) instead of appearing at once.
